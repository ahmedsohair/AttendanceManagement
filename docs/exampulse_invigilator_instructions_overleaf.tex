\section{1. What You Need}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\bfseries}l X}
Scanner link & \href{https://exampulse.xyz/scan}{https://exampulse.xyz/scan} \\
Access code & Your personal invigilator code, sent by email \\
Device & Any phone with a camera and internet access \\
Browser & Safari on iPhone, Chrome/Samsung Internet on Android \\
\end{tabularx}

\section{2. Sign In and Select Your Room}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Open the scanner link on your phone:
  \href{https://exampulse.xyz/scan}{https://exampulse.xyz/scan}
  \item Enter your invigilator access code.
  \item Select the room where you are invigilating.
  \item Allow camera access when prompted.
  \item Wait for the OCR scanner to finish loading.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{itemize}
  \item After signing in, you will only see the room(s) assigned to you for the active exam.
  \item Make sure the room name matches your physical exam room.
\end{itemize}

\section{3. Scan a Student ID}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Hold the student ID card under the camera.
  \item Place only the \textbf{student number} inside the red scan box.
  \item Keep the card steady for a moment.
  \item When the correct student number is detected, review the student details and tap \textbf{Mark Present}.
  \item If OCR detects the wrong number, edit the number manually and tap \textbf{Lookup} again before marking attendance.
\end{enumerate}

\importantbox{The scanner reads the printed student number, not the barcode. It is optimized for low-light exam rooms, but if scanning is difficult, use the torch button or manual entry.}

\section{4. Understand the Scan Results}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\bfseries}l X}
\textcolor{Success}{Present} & Student is in the correct room. Mark attendance. \\
\textcolor{Warning}{Wrong room} & Student belongs to another room. The system will show the correct room/zone. \\
Already marked & Student has already been marked present for this exam. Do not mark again. \\
Student not found & Student number is not in the published exam roster. Check the number, edit it if OCR misread it, and tap \textbf{Lookup} again. If still not found, contact the Team leader in the room. \\
\end{tabularx}

\section{5. Wrong-Room Students}

If a student is in the wrong room, the app will show the expected room and zone. Inform the room Team Leader and follow their instructions.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Use \textbf{Send to correct room} if the student should move to their assigned room.
  \item Use \textbf{Mark present in this room} only if the Team Leader asks you to record attendance.
  \item Wrong-room attendance is flagged automatically.
\end{itemize}

\importantbox{Wrong-room override does not change the student's official room allocation. It only records that the student was marked present in a different room.}

\section{6. Manual Entry}

Use manual entry if:

\begin{itemize}
  \item the student forgot their ID card,
  \item the card is damaged,
  \item lighting is poor,
  \item OCR reads the number incorrectly.
\end{itemize}

Type the student number carefully and tap \textbf{Lookup}. The system will perform the same attendance checks as a camera scan.

If the student does not have their physical student ID card, use the \textbf{Comments} field to briefly record how their identity was checked. For example: ``checked photo of student ID'', ``checked another photo ID'', or ``verified by authorised staff''.

\section{7. Quick Troubleshooting}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\bfseries}l X}
Scanner is still loading & Wait 20--40 seconds on first use. Keep the browser tab open. \\
Camera does not open & Check browser camera permission and refresh the page. \\
OCR is not detecting & Use torch, keep only the student number inside the red box, or use Manual Mode. \\
Student not found & Recheck the digits. If still not found, the student may not be in the exam roster. \\
Network issue & Refresh the page or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. \\
\end{tabularx}
